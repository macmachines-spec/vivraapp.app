Chat and calls

  • Calls that behave like callsAudio, video and group calls ring on a locked screen, even when Vivra is closed.
  • You stay in controlBlock or report anyone, remove a friend in a tap, and delete your messages for both sides.

Walk Together

Walk side by side, from anywhere

Start a call with a friend and head out. You each see the other's steps count up, so a walk becomes a shared goal.

  • Audio or video while you walkTalk the whole way, or keep it quiet and watch the numbers.
  • Live steps and distance togetherBoth of you appear on one screen with your own step count and the distance you cover together.
  • Share the finished walkPost your summary as an Aura, and keep a daily walk streak going.
No GPS and no location tracking. Walk Together uses your phone's step counter, so nobody sees where you are.

Auras and Vivra Meet

Meet new people, on your terms

Meet is a place to share, follow and connect, kept apart from your private chats. It is for people aged 18 and over.

  • Your own Meet profileAdd a photo and a backdrop, post, and follow people you like. Others can follow or connect with you.
  • Your Vivra code stays privateIt is never shown in Meet, so your private chats stay private.
  • Auras for friends and for MeetChat Auras go to your friends. Meet Auras go to your Meet circle. Both disappear after 24 hours.
  • Show or hide your Online statusSwitch it off in Meet settings, and report or block anyone at any time.
