    Introduction

    Vivra ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Vivra mobile application.

    We operate in full compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Romanian data protection law. By using Vivra, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

    What changed in this version. Vivra now includes groups, audio and video calls, group calls, Auras, Walk Together, optional sign-in, and Vivra Meet for people aged 18 and over. Chats, groups, Meet profiles and posts, and Auras are stored on our servers. Your habit and fitness data still stays on your device. The sections below explain exactly what is covered.

    Messaging & Calling

    When you use Vivra chat, your messages and any files you attach are uploaded to our cloud infrastructure, provided by Google Firebase, so that they can be delivered to the person you are messaging. This is different from your habit and fitness data, which stays on your device.

    Please read this carefully. Vivra does not currently offer end to end encryption. Your messages are encrypted while travelling over the internet (TLS) and encrypted while stored on our servers, which protects them from outside parties. However, they are not encrypted in a way that makes them unreadable to us. This means that we are technically capable of accessing message content, and we may be legally required to disclose it in response to a valid legal request. Please do not use Vivra to share highly sensitive information such as financial credentials, government identity numbers, or medical records.

    How the pieces work:

    What Where it is handled Stored?
    Message text Google Cloud Firestore Yes, until deleted
    Photos, videos, voice notes, audio, documents Firebase Cloud Storage Yes, until deleted
    Call audio and video LiveKit, streamed live between participants Never recorded or stored
    Call metadata Google Cloud Firestore Yes, as a chat entry
    Notification delivery Firebase Cloud Messaging Transient

    Call audio and video are streamed live between the people on the call and are never recorded or written to disk by us. Only the fact that a call happened, its time, and its duration are saved, so that both people can see it in their chat history.

    Deleting a message removes it, and any attached file, for both you and the other person. Messages you have already sent may remain on the other person's device if they have saved or exported them, which is outside our control.

    Data for chat and calling is processed on servers located in the United States. Transfers out of the EEA are made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

    Who Can Contact You

    Vivra does not upload your phone contacts and does not use your phone number. In chat, you are found only through a short Vivra code that you choose to share, and a connection is only created once you accept a request. If you create a Meet profile, other Meet users can discover that profile and follow you, but they never see your Vivra code.

    • You can block any user, which prevents them from messaging or calling you
    • You can remove a friend at any time, which removes the connection for both people
    • You can report a user for abuse directly from the chat screen or from Meet

    To limit unsolicited requests, Vivra restricts how many friend requests a single account can send in a short period.

    Accounts & Sign-in

    You start with an anonymous account. You can optionally sign in with Google or an email link. Signing in ties your friends, groups and chats to you instead of to one phone, so they come back if you reinstall Vivra or change phones. You need to sign in to use Vivra Meet. If you sign in, your email address is linked to your account and stored with Firebase Authentication.

    Vivra Meet

    Vivra Meet is optional and only for people aged 18 and over. When you create a Meet profile, the following can be seen by other Meet users:

    • Your display name, profile photo and backdrop (cover photo), and any details you choose to add
    • Your posts, reposts and comments
    • Who you follow and who follows you
    • Your Online status, which you can show or hide in Meet settings
    • Your city, only if you choose to show it. Never your exact location

    Your Vivra friend code is never shown in Meet. Meet content is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Cloud Storage. You can report users and content, and block users.

    Groups, Calls & Auras

    • Groups: members of a group can see its messages, files and member list.
    • Video and group calls: call audio and video are streamed live through LiveKit between participants and are never recorded or stored. Only the fact that a call happened and its duration are saved.
    • Auras: an Aura is a photo or short moment that is removed after the time you choose, 24 hours by default. Chat Auras are visible to your friends. Meet Auras are visible to your Meet circle (people you follow, people who follow you and Meet friends). You can see who viewed your Aura and who reacted.

    Other people can take screenshots of what you share, which is outside our control.

    Walk Together

    🚶 Walk Together does not use GPS and Vivra does not collect your location.

    During a Walk Together call, your step count and distance are shared live with the friends in the walk. Distance is estimated from your steps. When the walk ends, the summary stays on your device unless you choose to post it as an Aura or share it.

    Health & Fitness Data

    🔒 Your health and fitness data is processed and stored entirely on your device. It is never transmitted to our servers unless you are a Premium subscriber and you explicitly turn on Cloud Backup. It is never shared with anyone you chat with. The one exception is Walk Together, where your live step count and distance are shared with the friends in that walk, and a walk summary you choose to post or share.

    To power the fitness features, Vivra accesses the following on your device, only with your permission:

    Source / Permission Used For Leaves Device?
    Step Counter sensor Counting your daily steps No
    Physical Activity (Activity Recognition) Automatically detecting whether you are walking, running, cycling, or driving No
    Health Connect (where available) Reading heart rate, active calories, and distance No

    Distance and calories are estimated on your device from your step count and the weight you enter. You can revoke the Physical Activity or Health Connect permissions at any time in your device settings, and the fitness features will simply stop updating. We do not use this data for advertising and we do not share it with third parties.

    Notifications

    Vivra sends two kinds of notification. Habit reminders and streak alerts are generated locally on your device from schedules you set yourself. Message and call alerts are push notifications delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging, which requires sending your device a notification token. You can turn either kind off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

    Data Storage & Security

    🔒 Your habit, mood, and health data stays on your device by default. It is only uploaded to our cloud servers if you are a Premium subscriber and you explicitly enable Cloud Backup in your settings.

    Chat messages, attachments and call records are stored on our cloud servers because they have to be, in order to reach the other person. All data in the cloud is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest. Access is restricted by security rules that allow only the two participants of a conversation to read its messages and files.

    No system is perfectly secure. We use industry standard measures, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    Delete Your Vivra Account and Data

    This page explains how to ask Vivra App to delete your Vivra account and the data linked to it.

    Steps to delete your account in the app

    1. Open Vivra and go to the Profile tab
    2. Tap Delete account and follow the steps

    If you cannot open the app, or want some data erased

    Email support@vivraapp.app with the subject "Delete my Vivra data". Send it from the email linked to your account, or include your Vivra code. Tell us whether you want your whole account deleted or only some data, such as the messages and files you sent. We will respond within 30 days.

    Data that is deleted

    • Your profile and Meet page
    • Your Meet posts, comments, followers and discovery profile
    • On request, the messages and files you sent, your friend connections and your call records
    • Deleted accounts are permanently removed within 30 days

    Data that is kept

    • Your chat messages stay visible to the other person, shown as "Deleted account", until you ask us to erase them
    • Reports about abuse are kept for up to 12 months so repeat behaviour can be recognised
    • Data we must keep to meet a legal obligation

    Habit, mood and fitness data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it from that phone.

    Data Retention

    We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services.

    • Messages and attachments are kept until you or the other participant delete them, or until the account is deleted
    • Call records are kept as part of your chat history and are removed with it
    • Presence data such as last seen is continuously overwritten and is not kept as a history
    • Abuse reports are kept for up to 12 months so that repeat behaviour can be recognised
    • Auras are removed when they expire, 24 hours by default
    • Meet profile, posts and comments are removed when you delete your account
    • Deleted accounts are permanently removed within 30 days

    Aggregated, anonymised analytics data may be retained longer.

    Vivra is a mobile application. Unlike websites, mobile apps do not use traditional browser cookies. However, we and our third party partners use similar tracking technologies, including mobile advertising identifiers, local storage, and SDKs, to provide and improve our services.

    Tracking Technologies We Use

    Technology Type Purpose Can Opt Out?
    Local Storage Essential Store your habits, fitness data, settings, and preferences on your device No, required for the app to function
    Local Media Cache Essential Keep a copy of chat photos and profile pictures you have already viewed, so they display instantly and are not downloaded again No, cleared when you clear app storage
    Push Notification Token Essential Identify your device so messages and calls can reach it Yes, by disabling notifications
    Google AdMob SDK Advertising Serve personalised ads where advertising is active Yes, opt out in device settings
    Advertising ID (GAID) Advertising Identify your device for ad personalisation Yes, reset or opt out in device settings
    Firebase Analytics Analytics Understand how users interact with the app Yes, disable in app settings

    Essential Technologies

    These technologies are necessary for Vivra to function properly. Without them, the app cannot save your data, remember your preferences, deliver your messages, or maintain your session.

    • Habit, streak, and fitness data storage
    • Cached chat images and profile photos
    • Theme and language preferences
    • Premium subscription status
    • App configuration settings

    Advertising Technologies

    Where advertising is active in Vivra, Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier to display personalised advertisements. This helps keep Vivra free for users who choose not to subscribe. Advertising is never shown inside a conversation, and the content of your messages is never used to select ads.

    💡 Premium subscribers do not see advertisements and are not subject to AdMob tracking within the app.

    How to Opt Out of Ad Personalisation

    You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

    • Android: Go to Settings, then Google, then Ads, then Opt out of Ads Personalisation
    • Or: Upgrade to Vivra Premium to remove all ads entirely

    When you first open Vivra, we ask for your consent to use non essential tracking technologies. You can withdraw your consent at any time through the app's Privacy Settings or by contacting us at support@vivraapp.app.
